# Theme Specification: Health

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: Clean, clinical, and data-dense. Information must be precise and trustworthy.
**Intended Feeling**: Breathable, pure, exact, clinical.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Cool whites, sterile blues, and very clear semantic colors for AQI/UV warnings (Green/Yellow/Red scales).
- **Typography Direction**: Monospaced or highly geometric sans-serif for numbers. Strict alignment.
- **Shape Language**: Sharp but safe. Moderate border radius.
- **Card Style**: White cards on very light grey backgrounds to create subtle separation. Clean divider lines.
- **Icon Direction**: Medical/scientific iconography. Clean and universally understood.
- **Background Direction**: Pure white or very light cool gray.
- **Animation/Motion**: Instantaneous or very brief fades. No bouncy distractions.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Data-heavy. Focuses on dials, scales, and precise numerical readouts.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: Strict colorblind-safe palettes for AQI and pollen scales.
- **Example Use Cases**: Users with asthma, allergies, or health conditions needing precise environmental metrics.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
Feels like a high-end medical dashboard or scientific instrument.
