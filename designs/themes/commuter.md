# Theme Specification: Commuter

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: Fast, glanceable, and alert-driven. Information moving at speed.
**Intended Feeling**: Fast, urgent, sleek, urban.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Asphalt greys, bright transit colors (highway green, caution yellow, stoplight red).
- **Typography Direction**: Condensed, large, highway-signage style fonts (e.g., Interstate or DIN equivalents).
- **Shape Language**: Sleek horizontal bars and wide cards.
- **Card Style**: Minimal padding to maximize screen real estate. High density.
- **Icon Direction**: Solid, bold icons. Highly recognizable at a glance.
- **Background Direction**: Dark grey or muted city-scape tones to reduce glare during early morning/evening commutes.
- **Animation/Motion**: Fast horizontal slides.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Focuses on timeline views, rain probability graphs, and traffic/visibility warnings.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: Glanceable. Information must be absorbable in under 1 second.
- **Example Use Cases**: Daily drivers, train/bus commuters.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
Highway-signage typography and horizontal, high-density layout.
