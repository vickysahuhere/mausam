# Theme Specification: Beach

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: Organic, breezy, and fluid. Tied to the ocean and tides.
**Intended Feeling**: Relaxed, sunny, fluid.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Aqua blues, sandy beiges, coral pinks, and seafoam greens.
- **Typography Direction**: Casual, flowing sans-serifs. Relaxed tracking.
- **Shape Language**: Wavy, organic, extremely rounded. No sharp edges.
- **Card Style**: Soft, pillowy drop shadows. Floating elements.
- **Icon Direction**: Friendly, rounded line-art with beach/ocean motifs.
- **Background Direction**: Subtle sand-to-ocean gradients.
- **Animation/Motion**: Slow, wave-like, sinusoidal easing.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Focuses on wave graphs, tide curves, and circular sun dials.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: Ensure pastel combinations maintain minimum text contrast.
- **Example Use Cases**: Surfers, beachgoers, coastal residents.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
Organic shapes and ocean-inspired color palette.
