# Theme Specification: Fitness

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: High energy, focused, and contrast-driven. Built for the outdoors.
**Intended Feeling**: Intense, active, visible outdoors.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Dark mode biased. Deep blacks, dark greys, with hyper-vibrant neon accents (electric yellow, hot pink, neon green).
- **Typography Direction**: Bold, italicized, or condensed sans-serifs for a sense of speed.
- **Shape Language**: Slanted edges, sharp corners, aggressive geometry.
- **Card Style**: Dark surfaces with neon borders or glowing accents indicating optimal activity windows.
- **Icon Direction**: Thick, bold, active icons.
- **Background Direction**: Deep charcoal or true black to save battery on OLED screens during runs.
- **Animation/Motion**: Fast, snappy, high-acceleration easing.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Focus on timelines, peaks, and 'Go/No-Go' indicators for outdoor activities.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: Extreme contrast for outdoor sunlight readability.
- **Example Use Cases**: Runners, cyclists, and outdoor athletes.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
Dark mode default with neon highlights.
