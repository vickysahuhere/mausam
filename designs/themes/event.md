# Theme Specification: Event

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: Elegant, calendar-centric, and sophisticated. Planning oriented.
**Intended Feeling**: Sophisticated, calm, organized, premium.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Monochrome base (black/white) with elegant gold, silver, or champagne accent colors.
- **Typography Direction**: Elegant serif fonts for headers, clean sans-serif for dates/data.
- **Shape Language**: Refined, sharp corners or very subtle rounding. Thin borders.
- **Card Style**: Looks like a premium calendar or invitation. Lots of negative space.
- **Icon Direction**: Thin, delicate, refined iconography.
- **Background Direction**: Pure white or deep premium black.
- **Animation/Motion**: Elegant fades, slow reveals.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Focuses on extended 14-day outlooks, comfort indices, and specific date targeting.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: High contrast but relies on spacing/layout for hierarchy rather than bright colors.
- **Example Use Cases**: Wedding planners, festival organizers, weekend planners.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
Serif typography and massive use of negative space.
