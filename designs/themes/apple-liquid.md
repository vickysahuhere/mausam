# Theme Specification: Apple Liquid

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: Modern liquid/glass interfaces with soft translucent surfaces and depth.
**Intended Feeling**: Premium, fluid, minimal, deep.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Subtle gradients, blurred translucent backgrounds (glassmorphism), high contrast text (monochrome).
- **Typography Direction**: Refined, highly legible sans-serif. Varied weights for hierarchy.
- **Shape Language**: Soft, continuous curves. Large rounded corners (e.g., squircles).
- **Card Style**: Translucent surfaces with delicate white borders (light mode) or subtle highlights (dark mode) for depth.
- **Icon Direction**: Thin, elegant line icons. High clarity.
- **Background Direction**: Dynamic blurred gradients that shift slowly based on the time of day and weather.
- **Animation/Motion**: Fluid, subtle spring physics. Bouncy but sophisticated.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Widgets feel like panes of glass floating over a dynamic, weather-contextual blurred background.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: Ensure blur contrast meets readability standards. Support reduced transparency OS settings.
- **Example Use Cases**: General purpose. Recommended for users who appreciate premium, modern OS-native aesthetics.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
The heavy use of backdrop filters and translucency sets it apart.
