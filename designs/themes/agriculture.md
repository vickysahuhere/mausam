# Theme Specification: Agriculture

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: Utilitarian, rugged, and earthy. Built for the field.
**Intended Feeling**: Practical, grounded, robust, serious.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Earthy greens, soil browns, harvest golds, and clear warning reds for frost/drought.
- **Typography Direction**: Robust, slab-serif or highly readable geometric sans. Dense data display.
- **Shape Language**: Square, solid, blocky. No wasted space on flourishes.
- **Card Style**: High contrast, distinct borders. Designed to look like a physical field notebook or machinery dashboard.
- **Icon Direction**: Utilitarian, tool-like icons.
- **Background Direction**: Textured off-white (like paper) or solid dark green.
- **Animation/Motion**: Minimal to none. Focus is entirely on data retrieval.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Focuses on historical data comparisons, soil moisture graphs, and distinct risk flags.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: Must be readable in direct, harsh outdoor sunlight (high contrast).
- **Example Use Cases**: Farmers, gardeners, agricultural planners.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
Slab typography and earthy, functional color palette.
