# Theme Specification: Travel

## Overview
**Design Philosophy**: Structured, cosmopolitan, and organized. Inspired by passports and flight tickets.
**Intended Feeling**: Organized, global, structured, reliable.

## Visual Language
- **Color Direction**: Navy blues, passport greens, stark whites, and transit-yellow accents.
- **Typography Direction**: Traditional sans-serif mixed with serif headers, mimicking print tickets or transit boards.
- **Shape Language**: Rectangular with clipped corners (like a ticket). Perforated dividers.
- **Card Style**: High contrast borders. Distinct sections for different cities.
- **Icon Direction**: International standard transit iconography (AIGA style).
- **Background Direction**: Subtle grid or map-texture patterns on a light background.
- **Animation/Motion**: Slide and flip transitions, like a departure board.

## Widget Integration
**Widget Style**: Focus on multi-city lists, timezones, and compact warning badges.

## Accessibility & Use Cases
- **Accessibility Considerations**: Clear visual hierarchy and grouping for scanning multiple locations quickly.
- **Example Use Cases**: Frequent flyers, vacation planners.

## What Makes It Visually Distinct
Looks like a beautifully designed boarding pass or itinerary.
